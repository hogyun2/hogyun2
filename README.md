I am **Hogyun Kim**, a Ph.D. student in [SPARO Lab](https://github.com/sparolab) at Inha University, South Korea. My research focuses on **scalable and collaborative autonomy for multi-robot systems**. I work on **multi-robot SLAM**, **place recognition**, and **communication-efficient map merging**, helping robots perceive, map, and operate in shared environments under limited bandwidth and computing resources.

- 🔭 Current research: Multi-Robot SLAM, Place Recognition, Map Merging, and Communication-Efficient Coordination.
